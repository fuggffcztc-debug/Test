About Swift Loan Limited

Swift Loan Limited is a microfinance company in Nairobi that provides working capital solutions to both individuals and Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). We are a non-deposit taking micro-finance company that offers tailor made financial solutions to meet cash flow changes faced by its target clientele, both individuals and SMEs.

With over 13 years of experience in finance, we pride ourselves on swift loan processing, competitive interest rates, and flexible repayment schedules tailored to your needs.

Privacy Policy

Swift Loan Limited is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us such as name, address, contact information, ID number, employment details, and financial information necessary for loan processing.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process loan applications, communicate with you, comply with legal obligations, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Terms and Conditions

By using Swift Loan Limited services, you agree to these terms and conditions:

Loan Agreement

All loans are subject to approval and verification. Interest rate is 7% per annum. Loan tenure ranges from 1 to 24 months based on your eligibility.

Repayment

Borrowers must repay loans according to the agreed schedule. Late payments may incur additional charges. Early repayment is allowed without penalty.

Loan Rules

Eligibility Requirements

- Must be a Kenyan citizen aged 18 years and above

- Valid National ID or Passport

- Proof of regular income

- Active mobile money account (M-Pesa)

Important Notes

- Processing fee applies based on loan amount

- Loan offers are based on credit assessment

- Maximum loan amount: KSh 5,000,000

About Swift Loan Limited

Swift Loan Limited is a microfinance company in Nairobi that provides working capital solutions to both individuals and Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs).

We are a non-deposit taking micro-finance company that offers tailor made financial solutions to meet cash flow changes faced by its target clientele, both individuals and SMEs.

Our Mission

To empower businesses and individuals by providing accessible, transparent, and secure financial solutions that enable growth and prosperity.

Our Vision

To be the leading microfinance institution in Kenya, known for innovation, customer service excellence, and positive impact on communities.

Our Values

Transparency: We believe in clear and honest communication with our clients.

Accessibility: We make financial services available to everyone who needs them.

Security: We protect our clients' information and transactions with the highest security standards.

Customer Focus: Our clients are at the heart of everything we do.

Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: October 2025

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using Swift Loan Limited services, you agree to these terms and conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Loan Application

All loan applications are subject to approval. We reserve the right to decline any application without providing a reason.

3. Eligibility

To be eligible for a loan, you must:

- Be at least 18 years old

- Be a Kenyan citizen or legal resident

- Have a valid ID

- Have a verifiable source of income

4. Interest Rate

Our interest rate is 7% per annum. This rate may be subject to change with prior notice.

5. Repayment

You agree to repay the loan according to the agreed schedule. Failure to repay may result in additional charges and legal action.

6. Fees and Charges

A processing fee applies to all loans. This fee will be clearly communicated before you confirm your loan.

7. Amendment

We reserve the right to amend these terms at any time. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of amended terms.

8. Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya.

Loan Rules

Borrower Responsibilities

1. Provide accurate and truthful information in your loan application

2. Use the loan for the stated purpose

3. Make payments on time according to the agreed schedule

4. Notify us immediately if you experience difficulty making payments

5. Maintain updated contact information

Prohibited Uses

Loan funds may not be used for:

- Illegal activities

- Speculative investments

- Gambling

- Any activity that violates Kenyan law

Default and Consequences

Failure to repay your loan may result in:

- Late payment fees

- Reporting to credit bureaus

- Legal action to recover the debt

- Difficulty obtaining future loans

Early Repayment

You may repay your loan early without penalty. Early repayment may reduce the total interest charged.

Communication

We will communicate with you via phone, SMS, or email regarding your loan. Please ensure your contact details are current.
